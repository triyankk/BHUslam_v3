Drag: orbit · wheel: zoom · shift-drag: pan · axes: X / Y / Z

Green boxes are current map-point associations, not an independent accuracy measurement. Maps are separate local coordinate systems. A map shown during tracking loss is explicitly stale.

Online geometry sampled about every two seconds. Keyframe poses and landmarks are copied from the same map snapshot; later optimization can move them. Route edges break across known tracking gaps. Map-graph lines are spanning-tree and strong covisibility connections; magenta lines are actual loop edges. These are sparse visual landmarks, not dense surfaces or ground truth. No navigation data is sent.
